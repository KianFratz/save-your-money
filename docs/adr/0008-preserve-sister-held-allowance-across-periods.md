# Preserve sister-held allowance across period boundaries

Disbursed allowance that has not been used or returned remains a sister-held balance when its period closes; it does not automatically enlarge the next allowance. The user may later record its use, record its return, or explicitly apply it toward a later allowance to reduce the required new disbursement. This distinguishes money still in the user's account from money already given, preserves truthful history, and avoids silently rolling budget limits forward.
