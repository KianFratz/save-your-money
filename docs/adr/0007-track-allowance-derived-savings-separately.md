# Track allowance-derived savings separately from account balances

Savings categories retain cumulative allowance-derived balances across periods: Savings uses increase them and later withdrawals decrease them. These balances deliberately do not claim to be the actual balances or market values of GoTyme, MariBank, GCash, Stocks, or other named destinations, because those places may contain money from outside the allowance and investments may change value; this preserves useful allowance history without expanding the product into bank synchronization or portfolio tracking.
