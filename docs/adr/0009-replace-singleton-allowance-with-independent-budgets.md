# Replace the singleton allowance workflow with independent budgets

Save Your Money will replace its single draft/active sister-allowance workflow with unlimited, independently dated Budget cards. Each Budget is created manually for one received amount, uses either a payday-based monthly period or a 14-day biweekly period, owns its allocations and expenditures, and may overlap other Budgets; timing is derived from dates rather than activation state. This supersedes ADR-0005's single active cadence, ADR-0006's allowance-disbursement workflow, and ADR-0008's sister-held allowance model because the product is now a general-purpose, budget-only tracker. A versioned additive migration will preserve existing data, map compatible periods and records into Budgets, and retain incompatible account-only records in legacy tables outside the new UI.
